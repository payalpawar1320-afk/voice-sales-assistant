
# VoxaFlow

**Close deals with your voice — no apps, no laptops.**

---

## 🚀 Overview
VoxaFlow is a mobile-first CRM built for field sales teams who rely on calls and WhatsApp.  
It enables users to log leads, track deals, record interactions, and manage payments using voice or simple inputs.

---

## 🎯 Problem
- Sales happens on calls & WhatsApp, but data isn’t recorded
- Follow-ups are missed
- Traditional CRMs are too complex for mobile use

---

## 💡 Solution
A simple, voice-first CRM that helps sales reps:
- Log updates instantly
- Track leads and deal stages
- Manage interactions and payments — all from mobile

---

## ✨ Features
- 🎤 Voice-to-CRM logging (simulated)
- 📊 Lead management dashboard
- 📝 Interaction tracking with timestamps
- 💰 Basic payment tracking
- 📱 Mobile-first design
